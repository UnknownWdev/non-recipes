Description

A delicious dessert made with a flaky crust and a sweet filling, perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • Pie crust
  • Fruit filling (e.g., apple, cherry, blueberry)
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Roll out the pie crust and place it in a 9-inch pie dish.
  3. Fill the crust with the fruit filling and sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Dot the top with butter.
  5. Bake for 50-60 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
