Porridge

Home

Description

A meal easy made with oats and milk, not much to say, but it is a good start to the day.

Ingredients

  • Oats
  • Milk
  • Water
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Combine oats, milk, water, and salt in a pot.
  2. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  3. Reduce heat and simmer for 5-10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Remove from heat and let it cool slightly before serving.
